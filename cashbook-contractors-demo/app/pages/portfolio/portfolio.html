How these numbers are worked out

Delayed counts ongoing projects that are already past their target end date or are projected to finish late at their recent pace. Planned and actual progress are compared over the projects that have a Program of Works schedule, weighted by budget.

SPI and CPI are totals across projects: total earned value divided by total planned value, and by total cost. They are only as good as the progress and expenses that have been recorded.
