S-Curve: Planned vs Actual vs Forecast (%)

Planned follows the Program of Works schedule. Actual follows the progress you record. The forecast continues at your recent pace.

Financial S-Curve: Planned Value, Earned Value, Actual Cost

Earned value is the budget multiplied by the percent complete. Actual cost is the expenses recorded so far, including payroll, materials and cheques.

How these numbers are worked out

Variance is actual percent complete minus planned percent complete today. SPI is actual divided by planned: below 1 means behind schedule, above 1 means ahead.

CPI is earned value divided by actual cost: below 1 means you have spent more than the work done is worth, above 1 means under cost. Forecast final cost is the budget divided by CPI.

Monthly rate is the average progress gained per month over roughly the last 90 days. The forecast completion date continues that pace to 100%, and the delay forecast compares it with the target end date.

Numbers that cannot be trusted yet are left blank with the reason underneath, for example no Program of Works, no costs recorded, or less than two weeks of progress.
