About Cashbook for Contractors

Cashbook for Contractors is a simple desktop application designed to help contractors track project progress, income and expenses, materials, purchase orders, and payroll, all organized per client and per project.

The application focuses on simplicity, clear records, easy project management, backups, and useful reporting without unnecessary complexity. It is part of the Cashbook Family of financial software.
